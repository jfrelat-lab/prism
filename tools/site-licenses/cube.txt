Cube — cube_2005_08_29_unix.tar.gz, Wouter van Oortmerssen's last release of Cube (2005-08-29), as
SourceForge serves it (https://sourceforge.net/projects/cube/, project file
cube/2005_08_29/cube_2005_08_29_unix.tar.gz; 30,412,318 bytes,
sha256 f531fdeaa30e5af430b1c9c8eaeb44c92e349490d6235261368049e3915720c4).

THE DATA. The archive's own readme.html, License section, is the permission this showcase relies on:

  Cube is freeware, you may use Cube for any purpose as long as you don't blame me for any damages
  incurred, and you may freely distribute the cube archive unmodified on any media.

So the archive is served as published, unmodified and free of charge, and unpacked in the browser
(the site splits it in two parts for its host's file-size limit and joins them back byte for byte).
Its maps, textures, models and sounds keep their own readmes, inside the archive, unchanged; some
of them carry their own terms, and they reach the visitor exactly as their author's archive carries
them. Debian lists Cube as unsuitable for its own archive for reasons of free-software policy (mixed
media licences, modified network code in the binaries), not because the archive may not be passed
on (https://wiki.debian.org/Games/Unsuitable).

THE CODE. What plays it is Cube's source of the same release, compiled to JavaScript by PRISM. Its
readme.txt licenses that source under the zlib licence with one added clause (source versions may
not be relicensed without the author's written permission); in February 2022 Wouter van Oortmerssen
and Lee Salzman relicensed all Cube releases under the plain zlib licence
(https://spdx.org/licenses/Cube.html). The source's own readme.txt is beside this file.
